# Trappy Hats — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

[Support / Assistance](support.html)

Trappy Hats is made by Clement Gougeon, France. For privacy questions or requests,
contact [cgougeon.pro@gmail.com](mailto:cgougeon.pro@gmail.com).

## Progress saved on your device

The game saves your progress, heroes, equipment, gems and settings locally on your
device. We do not operate a server that receives your saved game. The game does not
provide its own cloud-save service. Your device's backup settings may separately
include app data. Deleting the app removes its local data; this does not delete
Apple's purchase history, Game Center records or any device backups.

For Game Center leaderboards, the game also saves the player identifier and survival
records locally, so it can submit the right records for each account.

## Advertising — Google AdMob

The game offers optional rewarded ads in exchange for in-game rewards. AdMob may
process IP addresses and approximate location, device and app identifiers, ad views
and interactions, app interactions, crash information and performance diagnostics.
These data support advertising, measurement, analytics, SDK improvements and fraud
prevention. The game does not request access to GPS location. Access to Apple's
advertising identifier (IDFA) requires your permission through Apple's tracking prompt.

Google's consent form is presented where required, including the European Economic
Area, the UK and Switzerland. Ad requests depend on the consent system allowing them.
Where available, change your choices in **Shop → FREE → Ad privacy**. You can also
change tracking permission in your device's privacy settings. Declining tracking does
not prevent you from playing; it does not mean that all ad-related data processing stops.

Read [Google's partner-site information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for its handling,
retention, international processing and privacy controls.

## In-app purchases

Apple processes payments for gem purchases. The game receives transaction information
needed to verify a purchase and deliver its gems. We do not receive your card or bank
details. Apple retains purchase records according to its policies.

## Game Center and co-op

Game Center provides authentication, matchmaking and leaderboards. If you use these
features, your Game Center identity and scores are processed by Apple, and your display
name and records may be visible to other players. During co-op, the game exchanges
player identifiers, player profiles and match messages with the other participants
through Game Center to keep the match synchronized. We do not operate a separate
match server that stores these sessions.

See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Game Center records
and privacy settings are managed separately from local game progress.

## Diagnostics and internal test builds

The App Store build does not include the game's internal run-log and export tools.
A Store build distributed through TestFlight does not enable those tools either.
Explicitly designated Internal or Debug builds may keep local gameplay and technical
diagnostics for development. These reports are not automatically sent to us; a tester
can choose to share a report. Apple and the advertising SDK may separately process
diagnostics under their own services and privacy controls.

## Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you
choose to send. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue and handle
related requests. Our email provider processes this correspondence. Please do not send
passwords, full payment details or information unrelated to your request. You can
contact us to ask about access to or deletion of support correspondence.

## Your choices and rights

You can manage advertising consent in the game, tracking permission in device settings,
Game Center through Apple, and local game data by deleting the app. Do not delete the
app as a troubleshooting step unless you accept losing local progress.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain
a copy of your personal data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent.
Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out. For data we receive through
support, contact the address above. For data handled by Apple or Google, their privacy
policies provide the relevant controls and request channels; you can also contact us
for help identifying the appropriate service. You may complain to your local data
protection authority, including the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/) in France.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal
information to us, contact us so we can investigate and address the request.

## Changes

We will update this page when the game's data practices change. The date above identifies
the latest revision.
