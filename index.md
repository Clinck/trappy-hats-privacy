# Trappy Hats — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

Trappy Hats ("the game") is made by Clement Gougeon, France. This policy explains what
data the game uses and why. Contact: cgougeon.pro@gmail.com.

## Data the game keeps on your device

Your heroes, levels, gear, gems, class cards and settings are saved **on your device only**.
We don't run servers and don't receive this data. Deleting the game deletes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows **optional rewarded ads**: you choose to watch one in exchange for gems or
packs. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device and app information (model, operating system,
language, app version), your approximate location derived from your IP address, ad
interactions (impressions, clicks) and the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), the last
**only if you allow tracking** in Apple's prompt.

Google uses this data to show, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud.
In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent
through Google's consent form before ads load. You can change your choice at any time from
**Shop → Ad privacy choices**. Ads are never required to play.

Google's policy: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

## In-app purchases

Gem purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple sends
the game only a confirmation of what you bought.

## Game Center

If you use Game Center for leaderboards or co-op, Apple shares your Game Center player name
and scores with other players, under Apple's privacy policy. The game itself doesn't store them
elsewhere.

## Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct or delete personal data and object to its processing.
Because we hold no personal data on our side, requests about advertising data go to Google
(<https://policies.google.com/privacy>), and you can withdraw ad consent in the game.
You can also complain to your data-protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

## Changes

We'll update this page if the game starts using data differently.
